How it works

Six agents. One thread.
No marketing speak.

Every post moves through the same DAG: research, insight, angle, draft, review, publish. You see every step and approve every gate.

01
Research
Scans the web, your uploads, internal docs.
02
Insight
Extracts non-obvious, evidence-backed claims.
03
Angle
Generates 4 takes; you pick the sharpest.
04
Draft
Writes in your voice with citations inline.
05
Review
Fact-checks every claim. Flags style breaks.
06
Publish
Schedules or pushes the reply chain to X.
